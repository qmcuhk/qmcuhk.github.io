Qisi has been awarded the <a href="https://www.uc.cuhk.edu.hk/staff-corner/staff-grant-funding-and-support/" target="_blank">United College Dr. and Mrs. Joseph W. N. Cheung Research Excellence Award 2026</a>. Established in 2018, this annual award promotes research excellence and recognizes outstanding academic staff members of United College at the outset of their independent research careers. This honour is a recognition of Qisi's research achievements and dedication to advancing condensed matter physics. Congratulations to Qisi and the Team!
