Our PhD student Samson Sze Tung LI, together with Qirui HE, Yiwei LI, and Yongxin HUA, was part of the team that won the <a href="https://wp.phy.cuhk.edu.hk/news/news-25-07-21" target="_blank">PLANCKS Hong Kong Preliminary</a>. The team was subsequently selected to represent Hong Kong at the PLANCKS 2026 Final in Eindhoven, the Netherlands. Competing against 54 elite teams from around the world, they achieved an impressive 17<sup>th</sup> place globally and emerged as the highest-ranked team from Hong Kong. Congratulations to Samson and his teammates on this outstanding achievement!
