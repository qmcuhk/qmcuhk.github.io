The 23rd National Conference on Condensed Matter Theory and Statistical Physics was held in Changchun from 7 to 10 August 2026. Qisi gave an invited talk entitled “Magnetic fingerprints of high-T<sub>c</sub> nickelate superconductors.” He presented our recent X-ray scattering studies of the magnetic properties of infinite-layer nickelates.
