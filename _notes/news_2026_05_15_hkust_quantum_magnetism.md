Qisi gave an invited talk at the “Frontiers of Quantum Magnetism and Correlated Systems” symposium held at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. His presentation, “Magnetic and electronic excitations of nickelate superconductors,” introduced our recent resonant X-ray and neutron scattering studies of infinite-layer and Ruddlesden-Popper nickelates. By comparing their spin and orbital excitations with established cuprate phenomenology, he discussed the magnetic and electronic ingredients of high-temperature superconductivity.
